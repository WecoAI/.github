# Weco

**Autoresearch: agents that improve what you can measure.**

Point Weco at your code and your evaluation. It proposes changes, runs your metric, keeps what wins, and repeats - a search loop over your own system instead of your own afternoons.

People use it on agent harnesses (harness search), prompts, ML pipelines and configs, model development, fraud and finance models, and GPU kernels (Triton/CUDA).

### Work with our ML researchers

Have a research challenge that matters to your team? Start with a pilot. No ready-made evaluation or chosen technical approach required.

1. **Agree on what success means.** Define the business metric, establish the baseline, and agree on the result that would make the work valuable.
2. **Explore the possibilities.** Develop and test competing approaches, guided by your team's expertise and feedback.
3. **Leave with evidence and a clear next step.** Take away findings, code, and evaluations, with a clear account of what improved and what remains unresolved.

Talk to an ML researcher at [weco.ai](https://www.weco.ai/) or contact@weco.ai.

### Run it yourself

    pip install weco
